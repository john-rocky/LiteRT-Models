# F0 — can Idol run on a phone GPU? (Galaxy S26, 2026-11-09)

One block of each heavy part, exported with litert-torch 0.10.0 from the real speridlabs/idol weights (DINOv3 from the
Comfy-Org repack, FLUX.2 VAE from FLUX.2-klein-4B), run on the Galaxy S26 GPU (SM8850, Adreno 840) with LiteRT 2.3.0
(Maven libLiteRt.so + litert-gpu libLiteRtClGlAccelerator.so) through the shared C-API runner (devicebench
npu_chain.sh, round dir `device/f0/`). Every graph was fully delegated (`Replacing N out of N node(s) with delegate
(LITERT_CL)`). Each GPU form matched its source module on the Mac CPU in float32 at cos 1.000000 (`scripts/f0_export.py`).

## Findings that shape the build

1. The converter rebuilt the full score matrix: a concatenation of the per-chunk `softmax · V` products was rewritten
   into one concatenation of all softmax chunks ([1, 16, 12294, 12294], 4.8 GB at fp16) and one product. Fix: transpose
   each chunk's output to token-major before the concatenation (`gpu_blocks.attention`); the exported block then keeps
   one [1, 16, 512, 12294] score tensor per chunk.
2. A single GPU submission of several seconds starves the phone's compositor and the process gets SIGKILL: during the
   VAE encoder's ~9 s calls `surfaceflinger` and the app in front logged `fence … didn't signal in 3000 ms`, and the
   runner died (rc 137) on its fifth call; the memory guard and lmkd did not act. The 0.75 s denoiser block ran clean.
   The app has to keep each GPU call well under a second or two (ML Drift `kernel_batch_size` / `priority` options,
   or smaller graphs) — to measure next.
3. fp32 costs 2.7× on the denoiser block; fp16 with fp32 accumulation costs 3.8 % and cuts the block's error 5.5×
   (rel L2 1.45 % → 0.26 %) → the ship candidate is fp16acc32, fp32 only where a part fails its line.
4. The VAE encoder (9 s for ~4.3 TFLOP, and the worst fp16 error: rel L2 10.8 %) is the weak part: its 22 GroupNorms
   reshape full-resolution maps ([32, 4, 1024, 1024]) with 49 TRANSPOSEs and reduce 4 M values per group. A
   per-channel pooled GroupNorm without the reshape is the first rewrite (speed and fp16 range together).
